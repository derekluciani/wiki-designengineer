People

A directory of design engineers and voices shaping the role. Practitioners featured in the wiki's sources, plus a curated "respected list." Individuals can graduate to their own page as coverage deepens.

Featured in sources

  • Mariana Castilho — designer-turned-design-engineer (Vercel, Pierre); creator of the UI Labs project; advocate of "build first, learn what you need" and sharing work in public.4
  • Karl Koch — practitioner whose "Tips for New Design Engineers" emphasizes the final 10%, the generalist shift under AI, and "build with vibes, ship with rigor."5
  • Raphael Salaja — gave "The Rise of Design Engineering" at MIT Startup Week; frames the role as an interpreter "fighting slop," and teaches taste via Study / Note / Build.6
  • Glenn Hitchcock & John Pham — Vercel design engineers; "bridge the design execution gap," "you don't need to be 50/50," and build reusable systems even for one-offs.7
  • Ricky Zhang — author of the Ephemeral Design argument; also appears on the respected list below.3
  • Matt Perry — creator of Motion; author of the Web Animation Performance Tier List.13
